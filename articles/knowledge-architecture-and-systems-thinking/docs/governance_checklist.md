# Governance Checklist

## Boundary and Scale

- [ ] System boundaries are explicit.
- [ ] Adjacent contexts are linked.
- [ ] Scale is documented for major objects.
- [ ] Domain, platform, and institutional scopes are distinguished.

## Objects and Relationships

- [ ] Knowledge objects are typed.
- [ ] Relationships are typed and directional.
- [ ] Vague "related" links are minimized.
- [ ] Relationships include provenance.
- [ ] Relationship uncertainty is documented where relevant.

## Feedback and Learning

- [ ] User feedback can become review work.
- [ ] Evidence updates trigger revisions.
- [ ] Repository failures can trigger article updates.
- [ ] Outcomes and audits connect to revision records.
- [ ] AI retrieval errors can improve metadata and governance.

## Assumptions and Mental Models

- [ ] Category assumptions are documented.
- [ ] Evidence hierarchy is explicit.
- [ ] Editorial framing can be reviewed.
- [ ] AI-generated relationships are marked provisional.

## Responsible Systems Design

- [ ] Marginalized knowledge is not erased by dominant categories.
- [ ] Sensitive or community-governed knowledge has access controls.
- [ ] Correction and contestability pathways exist.
- [ ] AI-assisted workflows preserve provenance, uncertainty, and human review.
