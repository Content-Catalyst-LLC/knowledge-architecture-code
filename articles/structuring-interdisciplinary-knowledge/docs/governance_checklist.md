# Governance Checklist

## Concept Governance

- [ ] Every concept has a discipline context.
- [ ] Every concept has a scope note or review flag.
- [ ] Method context is documented where relevant.
- [ ] Contested terms are labeled and reviewed.
- [ ] Alternate labels are included where useful.

## Crosswalk Governance

- [ ] Crosswalk relationships are typed.
- [ ] Generic "related" links are minimized.
- [ ] Equivalence claims are documented and reviewed.
- [ ] False-equivalence risk is reviewed.
- [ ] Crosswalk provenance is recorded.

## Evidence and Method Governance

- [ ] Evidence types are distinguished.
- [ ] Method assumptions and limitations are documented.
- [ ] Legal, empirical, archival, interpretive, and community evidence are not treated interchangeably.
- [ ] Synthetic data is clearly marked.

## AI Retrieval Governance

- [ ] AI retrieval distinguishes similarity from equivalence.
- [ ] AI-facing metadata includes discipline, method, evidence type, and status.
- [ ] AI-suggested crosswalks are reviewed before publication.

## Equity and Epistemic Responsibility

- [ ] Marginalized knowledge traditions are not treated as decorative.
- [ ] Community-governed knowledge is not automatically exposed or repurposed.
- [ ] Dominant disciplines do not silently define the shared vocabulary.
- [ ] Terms with political or historical baggage include review notes.
