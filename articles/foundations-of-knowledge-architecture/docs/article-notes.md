# Article Notes

## Article

Foundations of Knowledge Architecture

## Core Argument

Knowledge architecture is the design of intellectual infrastructure. It makes complex bodies of knowledge navigable, cumulative, interpretable, and governable by organizing concepts, relationships, categories, metadata, semantic models, research pathways, and maintenance practices.
