# Methods and Measurement

This folder supports methods for inspecting knowledge architecture.

## Possible Measures

- Concept count
- Relationship count
- Relationship-type distribution
- Degree centrality
- Taxonomy depth
- Domain balance
- Metadata completeness
- Orphaned concepts
- Underlinked article clusters
- Overloaded hubs

## Review Questions

- Are core concepts defined clearly?
- Are relationship types documented?
- Are article-to-concept mappings explicit?
- Is the taxonomy too shallow or too deep?
- Are any domains structurally isolated?
- Are metadata fields sufficient for retrieval and governance?
