# Article Notes: What Is Knowledge Architecture?

This article defines knowledge architecture as the design of intellectual infrastructure: concepts, taxonomies, ontologies, metadata systems, semantic relationships, article maps, repositories, and governance practices that make complex knowledge usable at scale.

## Core Thesis

Knowledge architecture turns scattered information into structured knowledge. It is not only a menu, taxonomy, folder tree, tagging system, database schema, or content strategy. It is the deeper design of meaning, relationship, context, and maintenance across a growing knowledge environment.
