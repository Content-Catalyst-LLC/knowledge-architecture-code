# Model Notes: Designing Knowledge Systems for Education

## Core Idea

Designing knowledge systems for education means organizing concepts, learning objectives, resources, assessments, feedback, accessibility, repositories, and governance structures so that learning becomes coherent, inclusive, rigorous, and revisable.

## Core Layers

1. **Concept layer**
   - concepts
   - definitions
   - prerequisites
   - difficulty level
   - subject area
   - misconception risks

2. **Learning-objective layer**
   - objectives
   - competencies
   - cognitive level
   - learning outcomes
   - standards alignment
   - mastery indicators

3. **Resource layer**
   - readings
   - lessons
   - videos
   - diagrams
   - datasets
   - simulations
   - practice tasks
   - projects

4. **Assessment and feedback layer**
   - diagnostic assessments
   - formative assessments
   - summative assessments
   - performance tasks
   - rubrics
   - feedback records
   - revision activities

5. **Accessibility and equity layer**
   - alt text
   - captions
   - transcripts
   - keyboard accessibility
   - low-bandwidth alternatives
   - language support
   - inclusive examples
   - prior-knowledge bridges

6. **Repository and institutional memory layer**
   - OER records
   - licensing
   - version history
   - revision notes
   - teaching notes
   - cohort feedback
   - review status

7. **AI-assisted learning layer**
   - source provenance
   - AI-generated resource review
   - feedback review
   - learner-data protections
   - human oversight
   - model limitations

8. **Governance layer**
   - quality review
   - accessibility review
   - privacy review
   - AI review
   - curriculum revision
   - institutional accountability

## Key Review Questions

- Are objectives connected to concepts, resources, activities, assessments, and feedback?
- Are prerequisites and learning pathways explicit?
- Are accessibility and inclusive design metadata included?
- Are assessments aligned with learning objectives?
- Is feedback connected to revision?
- Are repositories governed by license, version, review, and accessibility records?
- Are AI-assisted workflows labeled, reviewed, and accountable?
