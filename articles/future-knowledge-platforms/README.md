# Future Knowledge Platforms

This folder contains professional companion assets for the article:

**Future Knowledge Platforms**

GitHub article folder:

https://github.com/Content-Catalyst-LLC/knowledge-architecture-code/tree/main/articles/future-knowledge-platforms

## Purpose

This scaffold models future knowledge platforms as durable intellectual infrastructure: knowledge objects, article maps, metadata schemas, taxonomies, knowledge graphs, repositories, AI retrieval records, AI outputs, source provenance, reuse conditions, accessibility records, governance records, correction logs, platform-resilience checks, and stewardship workflows.

The examples are synthetic but designed for serious knowledge-platform, public knowledge-library, research-infrastructure, open-science, AI-governance, semantic-web, repository-stewardship, and civic knowledge-system analysis.

## Repository Structure

- `python/` — metadata, provenance, reuse, AI grounding, governance, accessibility, repository, resilience, and relationship diagnostics
- `r/` — platform coverage, trust-readiness, reuse-readiness, governance, and relationship summaries
- `julia/` — future knowledge-platform quality metrics
- `sql/` — schemas for knowledge objects, metadata, taxonomies, relationships, repositories, AI retrieval, AI outputs, governance, accessibility, revisions, reuse, and resilience
- `rust/` — CLI-style future knowledge-platform validation scaffold
- `go/` — lightweight platform readiness coverage utility
- `cpp/` — efficient diagnostic metric examples
- `fortran/` — numerical platform-quality metric example
- `c/` — low-level coverage metric example
- `docs/` — model notes, data dictionary, governance checklist, and runbook
- `data/` — small synthetic future knowledge-platform datasets
- `outputs/` — expected/generated outputs
