# Governance Checklist

## Metadata and Corpus Governance

- [ ] Corpus scope is documented.
- [ ] Knowledge objects include required metadata.
- [ ] Source type, date, version, and review status are available.
- [ ] Deprecated or superseded sources are flagged.
- [ ] Sensitive materials have access controls.

## Taxonomy, Ontology, and Knowledge Graph Governance

- [ ] Taxonomy terms include scope notes.
- [ ] Ontology classes and relationship types are defined.
- [ ] Relationship provenance is documented.
- [ ] AI-generated relationships are marked provisional.
- [ ] Taxonomy and ontology updates are versioned.

## Retrieval and RAG Governance

- [ ] Retrieval records preserve query, index, result, rank, and review status.
- [ ] AI outputs are connected to retrieved sources or citations.
- [ ] Retrieval ranking considers source hierarchy and context.
- [ ] Chunking strategy preserves meaning and document context.
- [ ] Retrieval failures become correction or review records.

## Human Review and Correction

- [ ] AI-generated summaries, labels, and recommendations have review status.
- [ ] Human review records are preserved.
- [ ] Correction records capture prior value, revised value, and rationale.
- [ ] Feedback loops improve metadata, taxonomy, retrieval, and source ranking.

## Equity, Representation, and Sensitive Knowledge

- [ ] Classification schemes are reviewed for bias and exclusion.
- [ ] Marginalized and community knowledge contexts are documented carefully.
- [ ] Sacred, sensitive, or community-governed knowledge is protected.
- [ ] Users can contest labels, summaries, or retrieval results.
