# Article Notes: Ontologies and Semantic Networks

This article examines how ontologies and semantic networks support knowledge architecture by making relationships explicit.

Core themes:

- Ontologies as formal models of classes, entities, properties, and relationships
- Semantic networks as relationship structures connecting concepts, articles, datasets, methods, and sources
- RDF-style triples as subject-predicate-object representations
- SKOS for controlled vocabularies and taxonomies
- OWL/RDFS for richer ontology modeling
- Knowledge graphs as implementation structures
- Governance, versioning, and interpretive accountability
- AI-assisted retrieval grounded in structured meaning
