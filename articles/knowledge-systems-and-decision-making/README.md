# Knowledge Systems and Decision-Making

This folder contains professional companion assets for the article:

**Knowledge Systems and Decision-Making**

GitHub article folder:

https://github.com/Content-Catalyst-LLC/knowledge-architecture-code/tree/main/articles/knowledge-systems-and-decision-making

## Purpose

This scaffold models the knowledge architecture behind decision-making: decision questions, evidence sources, options, criteria, actors, assumptions, decision records, outcome indicators, feedback records, governance records, equity context, review readiness, traceability, and AI-assisted decision-support readiness.

The examples are synthetic but designed for serious decision-science, governance, policy, research-platform, and knowledge-architecture analysis. They can be adapted to decision registers, evidence-informed policy workflows, organizational governance systems, research advisory processes, AI-assisted decision support, and institutional learning systems.

## Repository Structure

- `python/` — decision-object, evidence, equity, review, relationship, feedback, and traceability diagnostics
- `r/` — coverage summaries, object-type summaries, relationship summaries, and feedback diagnostics
- `julia/` — decision knowledge-system quality metrics
- `sql/` — schemas for decision questions, evidence, options, criteria, actors, decisions, assumptions, outcomes, feedback, relationships, and governance records
- `rust/` — CLI-style decision-system validation scaffold
- `go/` — lightweight coverage and traceability utility
- `cpp/` — efficient diagnostic metric examples
- `fortran/` — numerical decision-system quality metric example
- `c/` — low-level coverage metric example
- `docs/` — model notes, data dictionary, governance checklist, and runbook
- `data/` — small synthetic decision knowledge-system datasets
- `outputs/` — expected/generated outputs
