# Model Notes: Knowledge Systems and Decision-Making

## Core Idea

Knowledge systems shape decision-making by organizing evidence, options, criteria, uncertainty, values, actors, authority, feedback, and governance into structures that support responsible judgment.

## Core Layers

1. **Decision-question layer**
   - problem statement
   - decision context
   - domain or jurisdiction
   - affected population
   - status
   - review date

2. **Evidence layer**
   - evaluation studies
   - datasets
   - expert review
   - community feedback
   - model outputs
   - legal or policy records
   - uncertainty notes

3. **Option and criteria layer**
   - decision options
   - criteria
   - weights
   - scores
   - risk notes
   - equity notes
   - implementation notes

4. **Actor and authority layer**
   - decision authority
   - affected communities
   - analysts
   - reviewers
   - implementing institutions
   - accountability bodies

5. **Decision-record layer**
   - selected option
   - rationale
   - evidence used
   - assumptions
   - uncertainty
   - authority
   - date

6. **Feedback and learning layer**
   - outcome indicators
   - feedback records
   - evaluation notes
   - revision triggers
   - institutional memory

7. **Governance layer**
   - review status
   - access rules
   - AI review
   - equity review
   - revision history
   - audit trail

## Key Review Questions

- Is the decision question explicit?
- Are evidence sources documented and typed?
- Are decision options connected to criteria?
- Are equity and affected-group context included?
- Are relationships traceable?
- Are uncertainty and assumptions visible?
- Is feedback connected to the decision record?
- Can AI-assisted decision support be audited?
