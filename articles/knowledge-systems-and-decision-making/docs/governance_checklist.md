# Governance Checklist

## Decision Question and Context

- [ ] The decision question is explicit.
- [ ] Decision context, domain, or jurisdiction is documented.
- [ ] Affected populations are identified where relevant.
- [ ] Decision authority and accountability are recorded.
- [ ] Alternative problem frames are noted where useful.

## Evidence and Uncertainty

- [ ] Evidence sources are typed.
- [ ] Evidence quality and limitations are documented.
- [ ] Uncertainty notes exist for major claims.
- [ ] Models and datasets are not treated as judgment.
- [ ] Community feedback has consent and context where applicable.

## Options, Criteria, and Trade-offs

- [ ] Options are defined.
- [ ] Criteria are explicit.
- [ ] Weights or priorities are transparent.
- [ ] Option scores are linked to evidence.
- [ ] Trade-offs are documented.
- [ ] Equity context is included for relevant options.

## Feedback and Learning

- [ ] Outcome indicators are linked to the decision.
- [ ] Feedback records are linked to the decision.
- [ ] Review dates or revision triggers exist.
- [ ] Institutional memory is preserved.

## AI-Assisted Decision Support

- [ ] AI summaries include provenance and uncertainty.
- [ ] AI-generated decision-support material is reviewed.
- [ ] AI retrieval distinguishes evidence, options, criteria, decisions, outcomes, and feedback.
- [ ] AI outputs do not replace accountable decision authority.
