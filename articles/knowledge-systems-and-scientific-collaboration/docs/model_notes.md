# Model Notes: Knowledge Systems and Scientific Collaboration

## Core Idea

Scientific collaboration depends on structured knowledge systems that connect researchers, institutions, roles, research questions, methods, datasets, metadata, software, publications, peer review, ethics records, governance rules, and revision histories.

## Core Layers

1. **Project layer**
   - research projects
   - research questions
   - hypotheses
   - project status
   - governance notes
   - funding and institutional context

2. **Contributor layer**
   - researchers
   - institutions
   - contributor roles
   - authorship criteria
   - community partners
   - technical staff
   - data stewards
   - software engineers

3. **Data and methods layer**
   - datasets
   - data dictionaries
   - protocols
   - instruments
   - samples
   - field sites
   - collection methods
   - quality flags

4. **Software and computational workflow layer**
   - analysis scripts
   - environments
   - workflow logs
   - containers
   - tests
   - expected outputs
   - dependency records

5. **Publication and communication layer**
   - manuscripts
   - preprints
   - publications
   - posters
   - technical reports
   - peer-review records
   - corrections
   - replication records

6. **Governance and ethics layer**
   - ethics approvals
   - consent notes
   - data-use agreements
   - access restrictions
   - conflict disclosures
   - dual-use review
   - data sovereignty records

7. **Open science and FAIR layer**
   - persistent identifiers
   - metadata completeness
   - access conditions
   - interoperability
   - reusable documentation
   - license records
   - repository stewardship

8. **AI-assisted research layer**
   - AI literature search
   - AI metadata generation
   - AI code assistance
   - AI data extraction
   - provenance and review
   - hallucination/citation checks

## Key Review Questions

- Are datasets, protocols, code, outputs, and publications connected?
- Are contributor roles and institutional affiliations visible?
- Are metadata and provenance records complete enough for reuse?
- Are data, code, environment, and outputs connected for reproducibility?
- Are ethics and data-governance records connected to the work they govern?
- Are open-science and FAIR expectations documented?
- Are corrections and revisions preserved?
- Are AI-assisted research outputs reviewed and grounded?
