# Article Notes

Article: Research Frameworks and Analytical Models  
Series: Knowledge Architecture  
Slug: research-frameworks-and-analytical-models

## Core Argument

Research frameworks and analytical models belong to knowledge architecture because they make inquiry structured, inspectable, and reusable. They connect research problems, concepts, assumptions, evidence, methods, analytical outputs, validation practices, and interpretation.

## Key Distinctions

- Frameworks organize inquiry.
- Analytical models represent selected relationships for analysis.
- Methods produce, structure, or interpret evidence.
- Evidence supports or challenges framework elements.
- Governance keeps frameworks revisable over time.
