# Model Notes: Knowledge Architecture in Sustainability Science

## Core Idea

Knowledge architecture in sustainability science organizes concepts, evidence, data, models, indicators, policies, communities, places, institutions, and governance records so that sustainability knowledge remains findable, interpretable, reusable, justice-aware, and accountable.

## Core Layers

1. **System layer**
   - climate systems
   - ecosystems
   - water systems
   - food systems
   - energy systems
   - cities and infrastructure
   - institutions
   - communities

2. **Object layer**
   - articles
   - datasets
   - indicators
   - models
   - scenarios
   - policies
   - community records
   - repositories
   - governance records

3. **Evidence layer**
   - observations
   - remote sensing
   - statistical data
   - models
   - case studies
   - legal and policy records
   - community knowledge
   - historical sources

4. **Scale layer**
   - local
   - regional
   - national
   - global
   - watershed
   - ecosystem
   - institutional
   - platform

5. **Justice layer**
   - distribution
   - recognition
   - procedure
   - historical responsibility
   - community governance
   - affected populations
   - access conditions

6. **Semantic layer**
   - sustainability taxonomies
   - system relationships
   - concept crosswalks
   - knowledge graphs
   - SDG mappings
   - planetary-boundary mappings

7. **Governance layer**
   - data sensitivity review
   - model documentation
   - indicator definitions
   - community agreements
   - AI retrieval rules
   - revision records

## Key Review Questions

- Does each object have metadata?
- Does each object include scale context?
- Does justice context exist where relevant?
- Are relationships typed and traceable?
- Are models separated from datasets and indicators?
- Are community records governed responsibly?
- Are AI retrieval pathways grounded in metadata, scale, uncertainty, and provenance?
