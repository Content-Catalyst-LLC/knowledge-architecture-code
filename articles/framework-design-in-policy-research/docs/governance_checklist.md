# Governance Checklist

## Problem and Framing

- [ ] Problem definition is explicit.
- [ ] Scope and jurisdiction are documented.
- [ ] Affected populations are identified.
- [ ] Alternative problem frames are noted where relevant.
- [ ] Historical or structural context is included where relevant.

## Evidence and Causality

- [ ] Evidence types are distinguished.
- [ ] Evidence sources include method and uncertainty notes.
- [ ] Claims are linked to evidence.
- [ ] Causal mechanisms are documented.
- [ ] Theory-of-change assumptions are reviewable.

## Stakeholders and Institutions

- [ ] Affected communities are represented.
- [ ] Implementing institutions are identified.
- [ ] Legal authority and capacity constraints are documented.
- [ ] Accountability actors are included.

## Options and Trade-offs

- [ ] Policy options are compared against explicit criteria.
- [ ] Criteria weights or priorities are transparent.
- [ ] Equity effects are included.
- [ ] Implementation risks are documented.
- [ ] Trade-offs are not hidden behind a single score.

## Evaluation and Learning

- [ ] Indicators are defined.
- [ ] Baselines and targets are documented where appropriate.
- [ ] Evaluation questions are tied to theory of change.
- [ ] Feedback loops and revision triggers exist.

## AI Policy Research Governance

- [ ] AI summaries preserve evidence type, jurisdiction, and uncertainty.
- [ ] AI-generated framework components are reviewed.
- [ ] AI retrieval distinguishes evidence, commentary, law, community knowledge, and synthetic examples.
