# Article Notes: Conceptual Frameworks in Research

## Central Thesis

Conceptual frameworks organize the intellectual structure of inquiry. They clarify the research problem, define the key concepts, map relationships, document assumptions, connect evidence and methods, and support interpretation.

## Knowledge Architecture Role

Within knowledge architecture, conceptual frameworks serve as interpretive infrastructure. They sit between taxonomy and ontology: more analytical than a topic list, less formal than a machine-readable ontology, and more directional than a generic outline.

## Key Distinctions

- Conceptual framework: organizes concepts, relationships, assumptions, evidence, and methods for a specific inquiry.
- Theoretical framework: anchors inquiry in established theory.
- Analytical framework: provides categories or dimensions for analysis.
- Logic model: organizes inputs, activities, outputs, outcomes, and impacts.
- Causal framework: clarifies hypothesized mechanisms and cause-effect pathways.
