# Article Notes: Taxonomy Design for Knowledge Systems

This article examines taxonomy design as a foundational layer of knowledge architecture. It focuses on categories, hierarchy, controlled vocabularies, preferred terms, alternate terms, scope notes, taxonomy governance, taxonomy drift, metadata integration, search, AI-assisted retrieval, and interdisciplinary classification.

## Core questions

- What makes a taxonomy more than a list of labels?
- How should concepts, categories, and preferred terms be distinguished?
- How can taxonomy depth, breadth, balance, and orphan nodes be audited?
- How can taxonomy governance prevent drift and category decay?
- How should taxonomies support metadata, search, AI-assisted retrieval, and knowledge graphs?
