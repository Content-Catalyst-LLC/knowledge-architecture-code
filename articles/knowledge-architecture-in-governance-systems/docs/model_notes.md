# Model Notes: Knowledge Architecture in Governance Systems

## Core Idea

Knowledge architecture in governance systems organizes institutional knowledge so that authority, evidence, rules, decisions, participation, accountability, outcomes, audits, and revisions can be understood, reviewed, contested, and improved over time.

## Core Layers

1. **Institutional layer**
   - institutions
   - mandates
   - jurisdictions
   - authority notes
   - accountability notes
   - review bodies

2. **Rule and authority layer**
   - statutes
   - regulations
   - standards
   - procedures
   - guidance documents
   - interpretations

3. **Decision layer**
   - decision records
   - decision authority
   - rationale
   - evidence used
   - uncertainty notes
   - review pathway

4. **Evidence layer**
   - administrative data
   - scientific research
   - legal analysis
   - budget records
   - audits
   - evaluation reports
   - community knowledge
   - public consultation records

5. **Participation layer**
   - affected communities
   - public comments
   - stakeholder submissions
   - consultation summaries
   - response matrices
   - influence notes

6. **Accountability layer**
   - audits
   - appeal pathways
   - contestability records
   - oversight findings
   - public rationale records
   - correction processes

7. **Learning and revision layer**
   - outcome indicators
   - incident reports
   - after-action reviews
   - revision records
   - institutional memory

8. **AI governance layer**
   - model documentation
   - metadata
   - audit trails
   - human review
   - source provenance
   - contestability

## Key Review Questions

- Is authority documented?
- Are rules linked to decisions?
- Are evidence sources linked to rationale?
- Is public participation connected to decision pathways?
- Are accountability and review mechanisms visible?
- Are affected groups and equity context included?
- Are outcomes, audits, and revisions preserved?
- Are AI-assisted governance workflows auditable and contestable?
