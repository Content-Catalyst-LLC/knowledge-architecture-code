# Governance Checklist

## Authority and Rules

- [ ] Institution and jurisdiction are documented.
- [ ] Mandate or authority source is linked to decisions.
- [ ] Rule status and effective date are visible.
- [ ] Interpretation notes exist for contested or consequential rules.
- [ ] Review pathway is documented.

## Evidence and Public Reasoning

- [ ] Evidence sources are typed.
- [ ] Evidence quality and uncertainty notes are included.
- [ ] Decisions are linked to evidence and rationale.
- [ ] Budget and resource records are connected to decisions where relevant.
- [ ] Outcome indicators are linked to decisions.

## Participation and Contestability

- [ ] Affected groups are identified.
- [ ] Participation method is documented.
- [ ] Institutional response to participation is documented.
- [ ] Influence of participation is documented.
- [ ] Appeal or challenge pathways are visible.

## Accountability and Learning

- [ ] Audit records are connected to decisions or implementation.
- [ ] Revision records respond to audits, outcomes, or public feedback.
- [ ] Institutional memory is preserved.
- [ ] Follow-up status is documented.
- [ ] Decisions can be reviewed and corrected.

## Equity and Responsible Governance

- [ ] Distributional and affected-group context is included.
- [ ] Administrative categories and exclusions are documented.
- [ ] Sensitive data and community-governed knowledge have access controls.
- [ ] AI-assisted governance workflows preserve provenance, human review, and contestability.
